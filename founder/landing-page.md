## 8. Frequently asked questions

### Will the AI invent fake experience on my resume?
No. Huntly only uses the achievements, metrics, and skills listed in your Master Profile. It reframes and re-prioritizes your real work history to match the target job description without hallucinating titles or dates.

### Why do you export to DOCX instead of only PDF?
Applicant Tracking Systems like Workday and Taleo parse single-column DOCX files with higher fidelity than complex PDFs. You can open the file in Word or Google Docs, make last-minute edits, and export to PDF whenever requested.

### How is Huntly different from Simplify Jobs?
Simplify focuses primarily on browser form autofill and charges $39.99/month for AI tools. Huntly focuses on interview conversion: delivering tailored bullet points, custom cover letters, and hiring manager outreach for half the price.

### Can I cancel my subscription after I get hired?
Yes. You can cancel with one click directly inside your settings at any time. There are no contracts, no cancellation fees, and no annual traps.

### How is my personal career data protected?
Your resume data and contact details are isolated to your authenticated account. We do not sell your personal data or work history to third-party recruitment agencies or ad networks.
