Good questions.

Will the AI invent experience on my resume?

No. Huntly reorders and rephrases only what you saved in your master profile. It never adds employers, titles or numbers you did not enter.

Why export to DOCX instead of only PDF?

Many applicant tracking systems read Word files more reliably than PDFs, and you can still edit before you send.

How is Huntly different from Simplify Jobs?

Huntly writes the full packet for each role, including a pitch to the hiring manager, at a lower weekly and monthly price.

Can I cancel after I get hired?

Yes, anytime from your account. Your master profile stays on the free plan.

How is my personal career data protected?

Your profile is private to your account and is used only to generate your applications.
